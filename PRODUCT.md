# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Academic researchers, potential collaborators, recruiters, and students who want to quickly understand Xuyang Liu's research focus, publications, experience, and contact information.

## Product Purpose

Present a credible, current, and easy-to-scan academic profile. Success means visitors can identify the main research directions, inspect representative work, and find relevant links without visual friction.

## Brand Personality

Technical, confident, and approachable. The site should feel research-led and information-rich while remaining calm and polished.

## Anti-references

Avoid marketing-style layouts, oversized decorative elements, unreadably small research figures, isolated card grids, and visual effects that compete with publication content.

## Design Principles

- Make research artifacts legible enough to inspect, not merely decorative.
- Use hierarchy and grouping to help visitors scan dense academic information.
- Keep visual treatments restrained so evidence and links remain primary.
- Preserve consistent behavior across desktop and mobile layouts.
- Prefer coherent modules over repeated isolated cards.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain readable contrast, descriptive image alternatives, keyboard-accessible links and controls, responsive layouts without horizontal overflow, and reduced-motion behavior.
